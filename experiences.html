Home / The Atelier

Four fittings. One journey, built to fit.

This is the process behind every Trip Travvy itinerary — the same care a tailor gives a made-to-measure suit, applied to your holiday.

01
Consultation

We take your measurements

A real conversation — over call, WhatsApp or in person at our Vasai-Virar office — covering your dates, budget range, travel pace, dietary needs, mobility considerations, and the handful of things that would make this trip unforgettable for you specifically.

Budget mappingPace & interestsGroup compositionMust-see / must-skip list
02
Design

We draft the pattern

Our travel designers build a day-by-day route: flight options, hand-picked stays, transfers, and experiences — each chosen from properties and guides we've personally vetted, not a rotating list of commission-first partners. You receive a clear itinerary document with an honest, itemised cost.

Day-by-day routeHotel shortlistTransparent costingVisa & document checklist
03
Fitting

We adjust until it's right

Swap a city, extend a beach stop, tighten the budget, add a celebration dinner — we revise as many times as it takes. Nothing is booked until the itinerary fits exactly what you had in mind, and every change is reflected in the cost sheet immediately.

Unlimited revisionsReal-time cost updatesAlternate options
04
Departure

We stay on call, throughout

Once you're travelling, a dedicated Trip Travvy contact is reachable for the entire trip — for the flight delay, the closed monument, the sudden change of plan no brochure accounts for. Your journey doesn't end at booking; that's where our part actually begins.

24×7 trip supportLocal on-ground contactsPost-trip feedback loop
Why It's Different

A package tour fits everyone equally badly.

Fixed departures move at a stranger's pace, sleep you where the margins are best, and treat a 70-year-old and a toddler the same way. We start from your measurements instead — which is slower to build, but far harder to get wrong.

One itinerary, one client

We don't resell the same route to twenty different families.

Vetted, not rotated, partners

Hotels and guides are chosen for quality, and revisited often.

Costs, itemised in full

You see exactly what each night, transfer and experience costs.

Your first fitting is free — and takes twenty minutes.
