Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from a regular package tour?+

Package tours run on fixed dates and shared buses, built to a lowest-common-denominator pace. We start with a blank page and design your route, stays and pacing specifically around your group — nothing is templated or resold as-is to other travellers.

What's actually included in the quoted price?+

Every itinerary comes with an itemised cost sheet showing accommodation, transfers, listed experiences and applicable taxes separately, so you know exactly what you're paying for before confirming anything.

How far in advance should I book?+

For international trips and peak season travel, 6–10 weeks gives us the best hotel and flight availability. Domestic and off-season trips can often be arranged in 2–3 weeks.

Do you help with visas and documentation?+

Yes — we provide a full document checklist and guidance for visa applications on every international itinerary we design.

What happens if plans change mid-trip?+

Your trip designer remains reachable for the entire length of your trip — for a delayed flight, a closed site, or simply wanting to adjust the plan on the ground.
